    Terry’s OrderWriter

    0 of 5 steps read
    01

    I’d start with the fundamentals of the world after reading the boot blurb like DATA, Anomalies and Agent Levels.

    02

    After that, I’d introduce people to the main 3: Argo, Jane and Cathy.

    03

    Then I would have them look at trivia and/or mission reports regarding those three while making them aware that some trivias are continuations of mission reports or have linking trivias.

    Heads up from the writerTimestamps aren’t necessarily all that good right this second, so I’d probably ask them to please be aware that some events are mainly recognised through hints. Like a character recovering from injury or seeming less giddy as they usually would be.
    04

    After that, they could have free motion to see any other agents but I’d have them stay away from the Unclassified ones if possible and probably avoid mentions of Rarity, Gilgamesh or Echidna at all unless they’re reading on Jane.

    05

    Next, after they’ve explored all they want, they could look into some of the company things like Etiquette, the procedure behind hiring and whatnot.

    06

    After they’re well involved with the agency and some of its innards, they could start diving into other trivias and the Unclassified lore along with the deeper history of AnomEx and its motives.

    01

    I loved reading about the series’ Agents first. It left me wondering about the power system, which is when I looked at DATA.

    02

    After that I was curious about Anomalies, so I read about them.

    03

    Moving on from Anomalies, I immersed myself in the Classes and their DATA frequency criteria.

    04

    The mission reports are my favourite part. I’d read one, then do some digging of my own: a report, then a trivia of that character, until I hit a spot where I thought “this probably links to another trivia” or “this might be the aftermath of a mission”. Then I’d go and find that mission, so I had the context and knew the trivias around that character were set during or after that time.

    05

    After that I loved the world building so much, so I got to the filings, as Terry calls them, and worked my way from the Etiquette to the really in-depth hiring and injection process. It’s a good read if you’re into background stuff.

    06

    Then I dug through whatever piqued my interest. I liked the other Agents that aren’t really mentioned. My favourite is Wren lol. He’s cute.
